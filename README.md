# The Hollow Shift

A psychological horror mod for **Minecraft 1.21.1 (Fabric)**. The world reshapes itself
when you aren't looking, the game sometimes lies to you, and something tall and featureless
learns where you live.

## What's in it

**Gameplay**
- **Tension** (hidden, 0–100): rises in darkness, underground, at night, when you stand still
  too long away from home, and when you look at the Hollow. Falls in light, at home, in
  daylight, and when you sleep. The world gets slowly worse the longer you survive.
- **The Director** decides when scares happen: a daily budget that grows each day, forced
  calm after big moments, and silence before every confrontation. Days 1–3 are nearly quiet.
- **The Hollow**, a 2.5-block-tall figure with no face and no shadow. It watches from far
  away, stalks you only while you aren't looking at it, refuses to step into bright light,
  stands inside your base, and once in a while visits in broad daylight. It can't be killed;
  hitting it only makes it leave.
- **25 scares**, including torches going out behind you, a door left open when you get home,
  a chest someone sorted, a sign with a line you didn't write, wall torches that now point the
  wrong way, a cow that never stops looking at you, your own footsteps half a beat late, a fake
  "joined the game" message with your name, subtitles for sounds that never played, a fake
  "Saving world" screen, and burnt-out torches in your inventory.

**Visuals and sound**
- Fog that closes in to under 20 blocks and turns charcoal as tension rises.
- Screen effects: colour drains, shadows go opaque, the edges close in, light flickers, and
  colour fringes appear above 75% tension. "Peripheral decay" makes the edges of your vision
  rot for a moment.
- A low drone that fades in with tension, and a heartbeat above 80.
- **Hollow Shift: Atmosphere** resource pack (built in, on by default): muted grass and
  leaves, unsteady flickering torches, replaced cave sounds, and off-key cows, sheep, pigs
  and chickens.
- **Optional Iris shader pack** (`HollowShift-Shaders.zip`): fog, colour drain, vignette,
  grain and colour fringing that respond to how dark it is around you.

## Building

Needs **Java 25** to run the build tools (the mod itself runs on the Java 21 that Minecraft 1.21.1 uses). From this folder:

```
./gradlew build          (Windows: gradlew.bat build)
```

The first build downloads Minecraft and Fabric, so it takes a few minutes. Results land in
`build/libs/`:
- `hollow-shift-0.1.0.jar` → put in `.minecraft/mods/` along with **Fabric API**
- `HollowShift-Shaders.zip` → optional, put in `.minecraft/shaderpacks/` (needs Iris)

`./gradlew runClient` starts a test copy of Minecraft with the mod loaded.

## Testing commands (cheats on)

| Command | Does |
|---|---|
| `/hollowshift status` | Day, tension, budget, Director phase, where you are |
| `/hollowshift tension 90` | Set your tension, to see the fog and screen effects |
| `/hollowshift fire close_encounter` | Run any scare right now (tab-complete shows them all) |
| `/hollowshift debug true` | Print every Director decision in chat |
| `/hollowshift reset` | Clear your cooldowns and history |

Sleep in a bed first: your bed marks "home", and several scares only happen there.

## Settings

`config/hollowshift.json` is created on first launch. Highlights:

| Setting | Default | |
|---|---|---|
| `metaScares` | true | Fake chat, lying subtitles, fake save screen, inventory edits |
| `stealItems` | true | Whether a confrontation can take your torches and held item |
| `catchDamage` | 6.0 | Damage when caught (half-hearts); 0 = harmless |
| `photosensitiveMode` | false | No flicker, no colour fringing |
| `withIrisShaders` | "auto" | With an Iris pack active, the mod's own fog and screen effects step aside |
| `director` | | Every pacing knob (budget, relax time, silence length...) |

## Tuning the pacing

`./gradlew simulate` plays the Director through 20 days in 500 simulated worlds and checks
that its rules hold. Change `director` numbers or `DefaultEvents.java`, re-run, compare.

## Art and audio

Every texture and sound was generated by `tools/generate_assets.py`. They are placeholders
that fit the mood. Replace any file with your own (same path and name) to upgrade it.
